How to set up and use Carnival Live.

Set up your live feed

Select Edit Feed, then search for a club, association or competition. Follow a club’s teams or add whole grades and competitions to see their matches together.

Your selections are saved in this browser. A different phone, computer or browser can have its own feed.

Choose a display

Brief
Match details and score lines only.
Standard
Adds current batters, current/recent bowlers and completed team scores.
Detailed
Adds leading batting and bowling performances, including separate completed innings.

The selected mode is remembered on this device.

Live updates

Scores refresh automatically every 30 seconds. Match information depends on the scorer and the public Play Cricket feed, so innings breaks, results and other changes may take a short time to appear.

Install on your phone

Android: open Carnival Live in Chrome and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

iPhone: open Carnival Live in Safari, tap Share, then choose Add to Home Screen.

If something does not look right

  • Reload the page and allow one refresh cycle.
  • Open Edit Feed and check your saved grades and clubs.
  • Remember that a match may not show score details until its scorer starts or updates it.

About Carnival Live

Carnival Live brings selected cricket matches into one compact, mobile-friendly live view. It is independently developed and uses publicly available match information.

Cricket data, competition names and team names remain the property of their respective owners.
